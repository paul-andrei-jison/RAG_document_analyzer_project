Executive Summary

This report presents a consolidated overview of financial performance for the third quarter ending September 30, 2024. Total revenue reached $4.2 million, representing an 18% increase year-over-year and a 7% sequential improvement from Q2. Growth was primarily driven by enterprise subscription expansion and strong net revenue retention across existing accounts.

Revenue Breakdown

Subscription revenue accounted for $3.6M (86%) of total revenue, with professional services contributing the remaining $0.6M. Enterprise tier accounts grew from 42 to 61 during the quarter, each averaging $58,000 in annual contract value — a 12% increase from the prior period.

Profitability & Margin

Gross margin expanded to 71% from 67% in Q2, reflecting infrastructure cost optimizations implemented in August. Operating expenses totaled $3.1M, resulting in an operating loss of $0.3M — a 62% improvement from the same period last year.

"Cash and cash equivalents stand at $8.1M as of quarter-end, providing approximately 14 months of runway at the current burn rate of $580K per month."
